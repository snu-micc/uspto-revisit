This archive contains the product and reaction-role LoRA adapters supplied for
the llama3-8b benchmark reproduction in Supplementary Information S13.
Base model: NousResearch/Meta-Llama-3-8B-Instruct (base weights not included).
These are the checkpoints used for the supplied job-6285 predictions; this is
not a claim that they are the original Zhang et al. authors' released weights.

Extract this archive into benchmarks/zhang_et_al/ in snu-micc/uspto-revisit.
The adapters/prod/ and adapters/role/ directories will then contain the weights.
See the repository README for inference instructions and known provenance limits.
